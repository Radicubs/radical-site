# Radicubs film prompt for Google Flow

Use **one finished, silent 16:9 film** for the homepage section. Make the **robot the main subject throughout**. Upload several real Radicubs reference images: a clean hero view of one season's 7503 robot, close-ups of its mechanisms and wiring, a workshop photo with its student builders, and that same robot at competition. Select images from the same robot/season so Flow does not blend two machines. The website provides headings and controls, so the film itself needs no text.

## Main prompt

> Create a cinematic documentary film about one real Radicubs FIRST Robotics robot, team 7503, built by students in Frisco, Texas. Make the robot the visual through-line from the first frame to the last. Use the uploaded photographs as the source of truth for its exact frame, wheels, mechanisms, wiring, bumpers, team number, and colors. Begin with a close camera move across real mechanical details as students build and wire it. Follow the same machine through a short testing moment: mechanisms move, wheels turn, and students check the result. Transition by matching the shape and direction of the robot into a bright FIRST competition field, where the same robot drives into action. End on a strong, recognizable view of robot 7503 with students visible nearby. The robot must stay mechanically consistent across all scenes; do not morph it into another machine or season. Keep a photographic documentary color grade with charcoal shadows, natural skin tones, restrained Radicubs green, realistic arena light, and sharp detail. Camera movement should feel purposeful and smooth enough to scrub with a website scroll wheel. Hold the opening and closing compositions long enough to recognize the robot. 16:9 landscape. No spoken dialogue, music, sound effects, captions, titles, model-added logos, aircraft, generic sci-fi interfaces, invented awards, extra robot parts, or altered faces.

## If Flow needs separate generations

Generate three shots using the same robot references and style, then arrange **Build → Test → Compete** in Flow Scenebuilder and export one video file. Flow supports image ingredients, start and end frames, and arranging clips in Scenebuilder. Keep the resulting film around 15–24 seconds; leave a clear visual hold near the start and end for scroll scrubbing.

1. **Build:** Close documentary camera movement around actual students working on the 7503 robot. Show mechanical work, wiring, and laptops naturally. Preserve the reference people and machine.
2. **Test:** Track along the exact robot as its mechanisms and drivetrain are checked. Include students observing and adjusting it. Keep each mechanism grounded in the reference images.
3. **Compete:** Follow that same 7503 robot into a FIRST competition arena. Keep the bumper number and hardware consistent. Finish with the robot in a clear hero composition and students nearby.

## Delivery for the website

Export one 16:9 MP4 with no audio track, and provide a still first frame for the poster. The site can then use it as a scroll preview and offer a separate full playback control. A version with frequent keyframes will seek more smoothly during scroll; that encoding can be prepared after the final edit is supplied.
